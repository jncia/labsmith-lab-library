title: MPLS L3VPN with Route Reflector Core
---

## Goal

Build intuition for how VPNv4 routes move through a route-reflector core and how a route-target mismatch breaks customer reachability while BGP sessions remain up.

## Exercise

1. Verify BGP sessions on p1 with `show bgp summary`.
2. Inspect `bgp.l3vpn.0` and `CUST-A.inet.0`.
3. Break one PE by replacing `target:65000:100` with `target:65000:999`.
4. Confirm the remote customer route disappears.
5. Restore the route target and verify reachability.
